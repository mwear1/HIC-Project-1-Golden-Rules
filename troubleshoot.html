Troubleshooting

Answer a few questions to help troubleshoot your issue.

Is sound coming from the device?

Check if the power cord is plugged in. If the device uses a battery, try charging it. Then power the device on. Did this fix your issue?

Is the router powered on?

Power on your router. Try connecting again after verifying your router is operational. Did this fix your issue?

Has the device been cleaned recently?

Clean your device thoroughly and remove anything that could be blocking airflow. Did this fix your issue?

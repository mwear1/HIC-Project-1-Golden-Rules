How to Reset Your Password

If you forgot your password, use the password reset option on the sign-in page. Follow the instructions to verify your identity and create a new password.

Category: Accounts & Passwords

What to Do if Your Account Is Locked

Multiple unsuccessful login attempts may lock your account. Wait for the lockout period to end or contact the IT Help Desk if you still cannot sign in.

Category: Accounts & Passwords

How to Connect to Wi-Fi

Open your device's Wi-Fi settings, select the appropriate network, and enter your credentials if prompted. If the connection fails, reconnect and verify your login details.

Category: Network & Wi-Fi

Fixing a Slow or Unavailable Internet Connection

Check whether Wi-Fi is enabled, move closer to the router, and reconnect to the network. Restart your device if the problem continues.

Category: Network & Wi-Fi

How to Fix a Printer That Will Not Print

Check that the printer is powered on, connected, and has paper. Check for paper jams or error messages, and make sure the correct printer is selected.

Category: Hardware & Printers

What to Do if Your Computer Will Not Turn On

Check the power cable and outlet. For a laptop, connect the charger and allow the battery to charge before trying again. Contact IT support if the computer still will not start.

Category: Hardware & Printers

How to Troubleshoot Software Problems

Close and reopen the application, restart your device, and check for available updates. If the application continues to fail, record any error message and contact IT support.

Category: Software

How to Update Your Software

Open the application's settings or help menu and look for an update option. Install approved updates and restart the application if necessary.

Category: Software

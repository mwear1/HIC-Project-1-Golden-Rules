Help Articles

How do I connect to Wi-Fi? Network

Open your device's Wi-Fi settings, select your school or workplace network, and enter your credentials if requested. If it fails, reconnect and check that airplane mode is off.

How do I reset my password? Accounts

Use your organization's official password-reset page and follow the identity verification steps. Never share your password or verification codes.

What if my printer is not working? Hardware

Check that the printer is powered on, has paper, and is connected. Confirm that you selected the correct printer, then try again. Contact IT if the problem continues.

What if an application freezes? Software

Wait briefly and save your work if possible. Close and reopen the application. If it remains frozen, restart your device or submit a ticket.

Why is my internet connection slow? Network

Check whether other websites are slow, reconnect to Wi-Fi, and pause unnecessary downloads. If multiple devices are affected, report the issue to IT.

What should I do about a suspicious email? Accounts

Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments. Check the sender's actual email address and report the message using your organization's approved process.
